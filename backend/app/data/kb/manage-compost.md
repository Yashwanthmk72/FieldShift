# compost

Why: Organic carbon feeds soil life, holds water and stores nutrients.

Steps: Spread 5–10 t/ha of compost or manure. Leave crop residue on the surface rather than burning it. Add a cover crop between main seasons.

When: Before ploughing or in the off-season

Benefit: Steadily better soil structure and water holding
