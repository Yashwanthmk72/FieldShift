# drainage

Why: Standing water starves roots of oxygen and invites root diseases.

Steps: Walk the field after rain and mark wet spots. Open furrows or small drains along the low side. Delay sowing or choose a waterlogging-tolerant crop on persistent wet patches.

When: Within 1–3 days of heavy rain

Benefit: Healthier roots and fewer disease losses
