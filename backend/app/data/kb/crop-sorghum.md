# Sorghum

A heat- and drought-tolerant grain suited to dryland farming. Sorghum can produce grain or forage with less water than many cereals and is a strong option where rainfall is variable.

Uses: Food, animal feed, forage and bioenergy.

Typical days to maturity: 100-130.

Good previous crops: Cowpea, Groundnut, Soybean. Good following crops: Cowpea, Groundnut, Chickpea.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Sorghum midge; Shoot fly; Stem borers; Birds at grain fill; Striga (witchweed) in parts of Africa

Tips: Choose short-cycle varieties where rains end early. Thin seedlings to avoid competition. Bird scaring or early harvest protects ripe heads. Rotate with a legume to reduce Striga and disease build-up.
