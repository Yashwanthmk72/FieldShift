# scouting

Why: Catching pests early keeps treatment cheap and targeted.

Steps: Walk a zig-zag path weekly and check 10 plants per stop. Note leaf damage, insects and disease spots. Ask your extension office before spraying.

When: Weekly

Benefit: Lower pest losses and chemical use
