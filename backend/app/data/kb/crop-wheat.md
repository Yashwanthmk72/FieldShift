# Wheat

A widely grown cool-season cereal. Wheat benefits from fertile soils and well-timed moisture; include a legume break crop to diversify rotations and support soil fertility.

Uses: Flour, food, feed and straw.

Typical days to maturity: 110-150.

Good previous crops: Chickpea, Soybean, Cowpea, Potato. Good following crops: Chickpea, Soybean, Sunflower.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Rusts (stripe, leaf, stem); Aphids; Fusarium head blight; Septoria

Tips: Use rust-resistant, locally adapted varieties. Time nitrogen to tillering and stem elongation. Include a legume break to cut disease. Retain straw or incorporate it to build organic matter.
