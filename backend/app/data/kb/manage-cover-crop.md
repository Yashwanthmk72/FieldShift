# cover-crop

Why: Bare soil loses water and topsoil to sun, wind and rain.

Steps: Leave residue on the surface after harvest. Sow a fast cover such as cowpea or a grass mix in the gap between seasons. Terminate it before it sets seed.

When: Between seasons

Benefit: Protects soil and adds organic matter
