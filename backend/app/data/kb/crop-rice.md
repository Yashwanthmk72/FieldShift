# Rice

A major staple that can be grown in irrigated or rain-fed systems. Rice requires careful water planning; alternate wetting and drying may reduce water use where local conditions and extension guidance support it.

Uses: Staple grain and straw for livestock or mulch.

Typical days to maturity: 105-150.

Good previous crops: Cowpea, Chickpea, Legume green manure. Good following crops: Chickpea, Cowpea, Wheat.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Rice blast; Brown planthopper; Stem borers; Bacterial leaf blight

Tips: Level the field to use water evenly. Alternate wetting and drying can cut water use where local advice supports it. Use certified, disease-resistant varieties. Rotate with a dryland legume to break pest cycles.
