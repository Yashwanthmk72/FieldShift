# lime

Why: Acid soil locks up phosphorus and reduces root growth.

Steps: Take a local soil test to confirm pH. Apply agricultural lime at the rate the test recommends. Mix into the topsoil and re-test after a season.

When: 2–3 months before sowing

Benefit: Better nutrient availability and yields
