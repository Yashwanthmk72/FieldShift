# irrigation

Why: Gridded moisture is regional; a hand check tells you what plants actually feel.

Steps: Dig 15 cm in a few spots and squeeze a handful of soil. If it crumbles and will not hold a ball, irrigate. Water in short, targeted cycles, early morning.

When: Before the next irrigation

Benefit: Saves water and avoids stress
