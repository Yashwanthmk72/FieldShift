# Pearl millet

A hardy cereal adapted to hot, dry environments and sandy soils. Pearl millet has a short growing cycle and can provide dependable grain and fodder where other crops struggle.

Uses: Human food, fodder and dryland rotations.

Typical days to maturity: 70-100.

Good previous crops: Cowpea, Groundnut. Good following crops: Cowpea, Groundnut, Soybean.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Downy mildew; Head miner; Birds; Striga

Tips: Use treated, disease-free seed against downy mildew. Sow early in the rains for best yields. Intercrop with cowpea for stability and soil cover.
