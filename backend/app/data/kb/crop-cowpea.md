# Cowpea

A heat-tolerant pulse that fixes nitrogen and is well suited to many semi-arid regions. Cowpea can be grown for grain, leaves or fodder and fits well before a cereal crop.

Uses: Pulse, leafy vegetable, fodder and soil-improving rotation.

Typical days to maturity: 60-100.

Good previous crops: Maize, Sorghum, Millet, Rice. Good following crops: Maize, Sorghum, Millet.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Aphids; Pod borers (Maruca); Thrips; Bruchid weevils in storage

Tips: A good quick cover crop and nitrogen builder. Store dry grain in airtight bags against bruchids. Intercrop with cereals to use space efficiently. Pick leaves lightly so pod yield is not hurt.
