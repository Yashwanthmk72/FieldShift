# Chickpea

A cool-season pulse valued for protein and relatively modest water needs. Chickpea fixes nitrogen and works well after cereals, but is sensitive to waterlogging and some diseases.

Uses: Food pulse, export crop and soil-improving rotation.

Typical days to maturity: 90-120.

Good previous crops: Wheat, Rice, Sorghum, Maize. Good following crops: Wheat, Rice, Maize.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Ascochyta blight; Fusarium wilt; Pod borer (Helicoverpa)

Tips: Choose wilt-resistant varieties. Avoid heavy irrigation late in the season. Rotate every 3–4 years to reduce disease. Use well-drained loams.
