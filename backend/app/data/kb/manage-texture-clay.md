# texture-clay

Why: Heavy soil is easy to compact and slow to drain.

Steps: Avoid working soil when it is wet. Use raised beds or drainage lines for sensitive crops. Add organic matter to open the structure.

When: At land preparation

Benefit: Better drainage and root growth
