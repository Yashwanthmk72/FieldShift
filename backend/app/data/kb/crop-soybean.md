# Soybean

A protein-rich legume that can add biologically fixed nitrogen to a rotation. Soybean needs good drainage and adequate moisture during pod formation; inoculation and local variety choice matter.

Uses: Food, oil, feed and nitrogen-building rotations.

Typical days to maturity: 90-130.

Good previous crops: Maize, Sorghum, Wheat, Millet. Good following crops: Maize, Wheat, Sorghum.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Soybean rust; Pod-sucking bugs; Aphids; Cyst nematode

Tips: Inoculate seed, especially on new land. Avoid waterlogged sites. Keep weeds out for the first 6 weeks. Harvest promptly to limit shattering losses.
