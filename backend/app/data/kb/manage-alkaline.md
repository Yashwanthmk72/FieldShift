# alkaline

Why: High pH can lock up iron, zinc and phosphorus.

Steps: Add compost or well-rotted manure. Favour tolerant crops such as sorghum or chickpea. Test locally before correcting with sulphur.

When: Before the next planting

Benefit: Fewer nutrient deficiencies
