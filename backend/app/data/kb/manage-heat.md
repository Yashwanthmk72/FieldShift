# heat

Why: High temperatures speed up evaporation and stress flowering crops.

Steps: Irrigate early morning or evening. Mulch the soil surface with straw or crop residue. Avoid heavy fertiliser during extreme heat.

When: During hot spells

Benefit: Less evaporation and heat damage
