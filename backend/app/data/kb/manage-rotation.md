# rotation

Why: Repeating the same crop family builds up pests and disease.

Steps: Follow the rotation plan on the Rotation tab. Record what you grow each season. Keep at least one legume in every 3–4 seasons.

When: At each planting decision

Benefit: Lower disease risk and better nitrogen
