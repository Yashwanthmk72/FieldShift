# Potato

A productive tuber crop for cool conditions. Potatoes need loose soil and steady moisture, and benefit from careful disease management and rotation away from other nightshades.

Uses: Food, processing and market gardening.

Typical days to maturity: 90-120.

Good previous crops: Maize, Wheat, Legumes. Good following crops: Wheat, Legumes, Maize.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Late blight; Potato beetle / tuber moth; Bacterial wilt; Nematodes

Tips: Never replant on land that grew potato or tomato last season. Keep moisture even to avoid cracked or hollow tubers. Ridge soil up around plants. Use certified seed to cut disease.
