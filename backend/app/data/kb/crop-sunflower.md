# Sunflower

An oilseed crop with a deep root system and good drought tolerance once established. Sunflower can add diversity to cereal rotations and supports pollinators when flowering.

Uses: Edible oil, seed, bird feed and pollinator habitat.

Typical days to maturity: 90-125.

Good previous crops: Wheat, Soybean, Chickpea. Good following crops: Wheat, Cowpea, Chickpea.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Head moth / head-boring caterpillars; Downy mildew; Sclerotinia head rot; Birds

Tips: Wait at least 3–4 years between sunflower crops on the same land. Plant at moderate density for larger heads. Protect ripening heads from birds. Leave stalks as ground cover.
