# Groundnut

A valuable oilseed pulse that fixes nitrogen and grows well in warm climates. Loose, calcium-rich soil helps pod development, and dry weather near harvest supports quality.

Uses: Food, edible oil, animal feed and rotation nitrogen.

Typical days to maturity: 100-140.

Good previous crops: Maize, Sorghum, Millet. Good following crops: Maize, Sorghum, Wheat.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Leaf spot; Aflatoxin (storage mould); Aphids; Termites in dry soils

Tips: Gypsum or calcium supports pod filling on low-calcium soils. Dry pods quickly to avoid aflatoxin. Loose sandy loam helps pegs penetrate. Avoid following other legumes to limit disease.
