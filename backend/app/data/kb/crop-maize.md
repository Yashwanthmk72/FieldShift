# Maize

A versatile staple grain for warm growing seasons. Maize performs best in fertile, well-drained soils with reliable moisture during flowering and grain fill. Rotate with legumes to replenish nitrogen and break pest cycles.

Uses: Food, livestock feed, silage and bio-based products.

Typical days to maturity: 90-140.

Good previous crops: Soybean, Cowpea, Groundnut, Chickpea. Good following crops: Soybean, Cowpea, Groundnut.

Common pests and diseases (general list): Fall armyworm; Stem borers; Maize streak virus; Grey leaf spot

Tips: Apply nitrogen in split doses rather than all at planting. Ensure moisture around tasselling and silking. Scout weekly for armyworm from seedling stage. Follow with a legume to rebuild soil nitrogen.
