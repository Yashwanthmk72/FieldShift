# DEMO.md
Challenge: **Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data**. Team: **Project Earthwise**. Record the screenshots and video yourself from the deployed link (not done by the author). No NASA logos; no people under 18 on screen.

## 7-slide outline
1. **Title / team / challenge** — FieldShift, team, challenge name, one-line promise: "Climate-aware crop choices from NASA data."
2. **Problem** — Smallholders pick crops with little local climate/soil insight; weather is shifting; prices and risks are opaque.
3. **Field studio (screenshot)** — Globe -> zoom -> outline a field -> "Analyze". Screenshot: Field studio with an outlined polygon.
4. **User flow** — Outline field -> NASA POWER + SoilGrids fetched -> ranked crops with confidence + illustrative badges -> Risks & downsides -> Climate tab -> Market prices.
5. **How NASA POWER and SoilGrids make it work** — POWER: daily weather + long-term normals (~50 km cell, few-day lag) drive season fit and the climate charts. SoilGrids: modelled 250 m topsoil pH/texture. Be upfront about resolution limits (How accurate is this? page).
6. **Published market prices + risks** — Market page (monthly world benchmarks, daily India mandi if a key is set, "no data" where none) beside the Risks & downsides panel. Explain that prices follow each source's reporting cadence; they are not real-time or profit.
7. **Future impact** — Verify crop parameters against FAO EcoCrop and local extension, add local soil-test input, more country price sources (FAO FPMA), offline/low-bandwidth mode, local languages.
